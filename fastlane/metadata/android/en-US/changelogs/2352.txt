Synced with the upstream Pebble app: notification images and album art go to the watch when it asks, podcasts get seek controls, the weather app shows tomorrow hourly, and firmware from a file asks before installing. You can add your own app store source, fetched only after you confirm it, with no account token or search traffic. Every synced commit was checked in source and in the built app: none adds telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, sign-in or Google code, or undoes Gravel's hardening.
