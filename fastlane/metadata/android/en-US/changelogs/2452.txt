Turning off internet access for a watchface or app now covers more than web requests: Gravel blocks UDP sockets in its own process, and an app with internet access off runs in a sandboxed page set to allow no connections. Web pages inside Gravel lose HTTP/3 and WebRTC over UDP as a result. Changing the setting restarts the app's session. On Android 17, plain-HTTP connections to services on the phone itself are denied again, like all other plain HTTP.
