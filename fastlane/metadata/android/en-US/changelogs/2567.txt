Adds watch settings from Core's app: Quiet Time schedules, Notification Text Size, 5-minute heart-rate sampling, and Wind Speed in m/s or knots and a Night Backlight Color on PebbleOS builds that have them. Album art reaches the watch from players that share it only as an Android content link, and rotated notification photos show upright. The Memfault update check and diagnostics upload are removed. Also fixes dictations that could fail right after the speech engine lost its model.
