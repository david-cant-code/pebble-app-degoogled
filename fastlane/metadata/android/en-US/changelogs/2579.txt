Pebble Index 01 ring support, in early beta: pair the ring from the Devices tab, and its recordings go into the Notebook like watch recordings, without upstream's Index AI or cloud ring code.

Also, weather is now off until you agree to what it sends, and Current Location is sent as a nearby town's position instead of the phone's. Weather place search uses a town list built into the app, and Gravel no longer claims a cloud.repebble.com link.
